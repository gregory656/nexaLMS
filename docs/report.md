AI Academic Analysis

Instead of generic remarks, generate personalised comments for every learner.

Examples:

Excellent learner:

> Gregory, you demonstrated outstanding performance this term, particularly in Mathematics, English and Integrated Science. Continue maintaining your revision schedule and actively support classmates through collaborative learning.



Average learner:

> Gregory, you are meeting expectations in most learning areas. However, your Mathematics performance is currently at ME2 while English is EE2. Focusing on consistent practice in Mathematics could significantly improve your overall mean score next term.



Weak learner:

> Gregory, although you participated consistently during the assessment period, your Mathematics performance was BE2 while English was AE1. More daily practice, homework completion and seeking assistance from your teachers will help you improve. Continue working hard.
